The image comes from a fixed camera looking down at a table. It is 640 pixels wide and 480 high; u is the column, counted from the left edge, and v is the row, counted from the top edge. A robot arm at the bottom of the image will be told to carry out the instruction you are given.

Mark two points by calling the mark tool once:

- grasp: the centre of the object the instruction says to pick up, where it lies on the table.
- release: the centre of the opening of the container the instruction names.
